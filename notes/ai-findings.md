## Prompting and review lessons (derived from these observations)

Supply the immutable assignment plus concrete API/ADR contracts before asking for a design; explicitly distinguish public JSON numbers from internal decimal strings, calendar dates from timestamps, and name-based empty PRs from resource lookup. Ask agents to challenge the previous answer and compare candidate cursor tuples against ordering and concurrency guarantees. Require path/line, source session, tests and DB constraints for severity claims; invite independent reviewers to contradict **Pi1**. Ask for smallest changes and independent re-verification; don't reward speculative infrastructure or convert docs corrections into runtime PASS.

**Standing rule:** every future Pi/Cursor reviewer can challenge any orchestrator claim; append each *new, evidenced* hallucination, misread, over-engineered suggestion or conflicting recommendation here with source/time, short claim, evidence, affected requirement/contract, human/lead correction, disposition (`CORRECTED`, `REJECTED`, `DEFERRED`), final-case strength and file/commit evidence **before Pi1 disposition**. Reviewers remain read-only in this staged rotation: submit a bounded ledger-ready entry to Pi1; Pi1 appends it and corrects stale coordination claims. If provenance is missing, ask for it rather than manufacturing a case. `AI_WORKFLOW.md` later needs ≥2 genuine wrong/suboptimal outputs with human corrections, ≥1 genuinely rejected suggestion with reason, and actual prompting practices; this ledger supports but does not deliver or verify that document.
